\section*{Overview}
Marble Matchup is an abstract strategy game for two players that can be played in about thirty minutes.

To play, you will manipulate an inclined grid of colorful marbles.
When you create a line of three or more adjacent marbles of the same color, you will collect one of those marbles.
Then, the remaining marbles will roll down to fill the resulting gap in the grid.

You will use the marbles that you collect to compete with your opponent in a series of \emph{clashes}.
Each time you defeat your opponent in a clash, you will earn points.
The number of points you earn depends on how many marbles your opponent committed to that clash before they lost.

If you are the first player to earn twenty-five points, then you win the game.
