\section*{Gameplay}
You and your opponent will take turns as the active player.
Each turn consists of three phases: mix, collect, and score.

\textbf{Note:} On your turn you will manipulate the inclined grid of marbles in various ways.
At all times, each column of the grid must have between two and seven marbles in it.

\subsection*{Mix}
During the mix phase of your turn, choose one of the six marbles on the bottom row of the inclined marble grid.
Remove that marble from the grid. Then, insert that marble into any position in the grid.

%\textbf{Note:} You may not remove a marble from a column that has only two marbles in it.
%
%\textbf{Note:} You may not insert a marble into a column that has seven marbles in it.

\newpage

\subsection*{Collect}
During the collect phase of your turn, you will collect marbles from the grid.

If there are any lines (vertical, horizontal, or diagonal) of three or more adjacent marbles of the same color in the grid, collect one of those marbles by removing it from the grid.

Place the marble that you collected onto a clash tile.
You must place your collected marble into a hole that matches its color and is on your half of one of the clash tiles.

Collect additional marbles until you either complete a clash tile (i.e. you have placed marbles in all three holes on your side) or there are no more marbles in the grid that you can collect.

%Then, proceed to the score phase.

\textbf{Note:} You may not collect a marble that you cannot place on your half of a clash tile.

%\textbf{Note:} You may not collect a marble from a column that has only two marbles in it.

\textbf{Note:} If you can collect a marble during this phase, then you must do so. 

\newpage

\subsection*{Score}
During the score phase of your turn, if you completed a clash tile during the collect phase, you will score points and then replace that tile with a new one from the supply.

First, score a number of points that depends on how many marbles your opponent placed on that clash tile before you completed it as described by the table below.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrrr}\toprule
Marbles & 0 & 1 & 2\\\midrule
Points & 3 & 5 & 8\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Then, return all of the marbles that were placed on the completed clash tile to the marble grid (see \textbf{Returning Marbles}).

Finally, discard the clash tile that you completed and replace it with the next clash tile from supply. 

\newpage

\subsection*{Returning Marbles}
After you score a clash tile, you will return any marbles that were placed on that clash tile before you completed it to the marble tray.

When you return marbles, return them one at a time in any order you choose.

Marbles are always returned by placing them at the top of a column of the marble grid.

You must return each marble to the column with the fewest marbles in it.
If multiple columns are tied for having the fewest marbles in them, you may return a marble to any of those columns.
