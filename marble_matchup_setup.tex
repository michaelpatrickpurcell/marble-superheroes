\section*{Set Up}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item Give each player one score board.
\item Give each player both scoring tokens of a single color (white or black).
\item Place the inclined marble tray in the middle of the play area.
\item Place the marbles in the inclined marble tray in a grid of six rows and six columns. Ensure that there are no lines (vertical, horizontal, or diagonal) of three or more adjacent marbles of the same color. 
\item Place three random clash tiles in the play area between you and your opponent.

Orient each clash tile so that it is clear which set of holes is closer to, and therefore belongs to, each player.

\item Shuffle the remaining clash tiles and place them face up in a single stack to one side of the play area.
\end{itemize}
