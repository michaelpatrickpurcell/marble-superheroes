\section*{Components}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item 36 marbles
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, nosep]
\vspace{-1ex}
\item 6 red marbles
\item 6 yellow marbles
\item 6 green marbles
\item 6 blue marbles
\item 6 white marbles
\item 6 black marbles
\end{itemize}
\item 1 inclined marble tray
\item 10 clash tiles

Each clash tile has two sets of three holes in it, one set on each half of the tile.
Each hole is associated with one of the six marble colors listed above.

\item 2 score boards
\item 4 scoring tokens
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, nosep]
\vspace{-1ex}
\item 2 white scoring tokens
\item 2 black scoring tokens
\end{itemize}
\end{itemize}
